How It Works

01
You Apply
Tell us about your brand — who you are, what you make, and why it belongs on NØWN. We read every application personally.

Our team reviews against our curation standards. We look for originality, quality, and a genuine point of view. No exceptions.
03

Approved brands get a full storefront, access to our community, and a place in the Discover feed to tell your story.
